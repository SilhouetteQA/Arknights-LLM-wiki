Number four, major festivals across terra.
Different countries of terra follow their own calendars,
so new years might fall on different days depending on where you are.
But for most, it is the ultimate out with the old,
in with the new moment of the year.
From Ursa's to Columbia, Bolivar to Victoria.
It's all about gathering with family and friends
to share fine food and wine and exchange the warmest wishes.
In Laterano, everyone celebrates Christmas at the end of each year.
To honor the legendary messenger in red,
the sancta prepare endless desserts and drinks,
partying for at least two months.
Yanis people mark the year with specific dates
based on crop growth cycles.
On these days, families hold banquets
to show gratitude to the land mother.
The Sammy Feud value their bond with the grounds they walk upon.
They regularly perform ceremonies to connect with their ancestral spirits.
These rituals stretch over weeks
and people will lay out huge spreads in the snow field.
They're more than just ceremonies,
they're also times for revelry.
For the Litanians, art is everything.
Alongside the annual Das Kaiser Innsfest,
their city-wide music concert is a highlight of the year.
On this day, feasts and performances
fill the largest music plazas in every region.
Of course, we can't forget the grand final of the Kazimierz Major,
the wild carnival of Syracusa,
Maui Zotia, chief election gathering of Aqewala,
in the tropical land of Sargon,
and the Saintess Coordination Ceremony in Ure.
These festivals across Terra
are a moment to lose ourselves in celebration
and a well-deserved reward for everyone who gives their all in life.
